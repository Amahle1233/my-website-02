Crafted for the Everyday.

Established in 2001 and based in Durban, Team Clothing and Gifts has become one of the leading uniform and promotional goods suppliers in South Africa. We specialise in designing, manufacturing, warehousing and distributing goods throughout Africa.

How We Started

Founded with a simple mission: strip away the unnecessary and focus on what matters most—fit, fabric, and longevity.

Every piece in our collection is prototyped in-house, tested across real-world environments, and manufactured in partnership with ethical mills using sustainable textiles.

Pillars of Our Craft

Sustainable Fabrics

We source organic cottons, recycled synthetics, and low-impact dyes to minimize our footprint.

Ethical Production

Every garment is assembled in fair-wage facilities audited for safety and worker well-being.

Timeless Design

We prioritize versatile silhouettes over fast-fashion trends so your wardrobe lasts longer.
