Crafted for the everyday

Established in 2001 and based in Durban, we are a leading uniform and promotional goods supplier. We design, manufacture, warehouse and distribute throughout Africa.

How we started

We began with a simple mission: focus on fit, fabric and longevity.

Every piece is prototyped in-house, tested in real working conditions and made with ethical mills using sustainable textiles.

Pillars of our craft

Sustainable fabrics

Organic cottons, recycled synthetics and low-impact dyes.

Ethical production

Garments assembled in fair-wage, audited facilities.

Timeless design

Versatile styles that last longer than trends.
